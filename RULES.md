## Deadline and submission
- **Submissions close 10:00 AM Sat Oct 10, no extensions. The code freezes at the deadline.** Submit on the Cerebral Valley event page.
- The GitHub repo must be **public** by the deadline.
- Demo video: about **1 minute**.
- Post the project on X or LinkedIn, tagging Cognition/Devin, with #AppBuildersPH.
- Give the final team name, the members, and each member's role and contributions.
- Disclose the AI tools used.
- A live deployment isn't required if the repo tells the judges how to recreate it.

## Hard rules (a break = disqualification or a disputed result)
- Only participants on the official list can compete. A team that submits with someone not on the list is disqualified.
- One person = one team. One team = one project.
- Build the project from scratch during the hackathon.
- No outside help: participants proven to have received help from people outside the hackathon are disqualified.
- Results can be disputed for a pre-existing project, external help or faked benchmarks, among other breaks.
- AI coding tools and Devin are allowed; disclose what you used.

## Demo Day — Sat Oct 10, Cyberzone SM Makati
- 12:00 PM registration. At least one team member must be on-site by noon to be a finalist; no remote pitching.
- 1:00 PM: 10–15 finalists announced · 1:40 PM: pitches · 5:45 PM: awards · 7:00 PM: end.
- 5 minutes of pitch and live demo, then 3 minutes of judge Q&A. Show a working product, not a lot of slides.
- 10 judges, revealed on the day. Expect technical questions: how it works, decisions, architecture, the AI implementation, its limitations, and what each person built. They may review the code.
- Bring our own laptop. Wi-Fi, power, HDMI and USB-C are provided.
- People's Choice: the audience votes by QR code at the venue.
