Laptop: municipal view

Screens 16 to 20 of the brief at 1280 × 800, routes /municipal, /municipal/plan, /municipal/log. Same tokens as the phone. Offline too: QR scanning, merge, rules and the optional writing AI all run on this laptop. Sample data for week 2026-W41 (Oct 5 to 11). Writing-AI sizes are placeholders until measured. Design system

P2-L

Pass 2 · The laptop as a live hub

In the room, most people see the laptop. Each QR comes in, gets checked, merges, becomes a plan, gets approved and goes back. LaptopNav v2 carries the I1 tile; the new LoopStrip sits under the h1 and sub line on every /municipal screen. Sample times follow the demo: four barangays in by 9:20 AM, Maligaya-D lands at 10:48 AM, approval at 10:52 AM, the phone receives at 11:05 AM.

16–17

Municipal home and scanning

Five slots, one per barangay. Scanning keeps the webcam on between QRs; each result shows as a banner above the camera. A newer export from the same barangay replaces the older one.

18

Merged view

Totals add the age bands, so any column that includes “<5” cells shows a range (formatRange, en dash). The priority row is tinted and labeled; the reason is spelled out under the table.

19

Plan: rules first, AI wording beside it

The rule-based plan is always there and decides everything. The AI panel only drafts wording from the plan's numbers; every number in the draft is checked against the plan and marked. Panel states 19b–19d replace the right column only.

20

Approval log

Who approved what and when, on this laptop only. Approvers are roles, never personal names. Entries can't be edited; a new plan makes a new entry. Empty state: 20b.
